## Meeting Purpose

The purpose of this meeting was to review the team's progress on Sprint 4, discuss the current status of backend development and frontend testing, identify remaining tasks, and coordinate the next steps for developing the Admin Panel.

---

## Main Discussion

During the meeting, the team discussed the implementation of product, inventory, and customer order management features, the remaining customer account management functionality, completion of automated frontend tests, preparation of the test report, and the planned handover of the backend for frontend development and integration.

---

## Team Progress

### Backend Development

**Assigned to:** Salma Rahman

The backend developer reported that product management, inventory management, and customer order management had been implemented, including validation, stock status detection, order filtering, and administrator authorization. The remaining task is customer account management, followed by backend testing and verification of database operations and permissions. The developer plans to deliver the backend to the frontend developer once these tasks are completed.

### Frontend Development and Testing

**Assigned to:** Faezah Ahmadi

The frontend developer reported that automated frontend tests had been completed using Jest, jsdom, and Testing Library. The next task is to prepare and submit the Sprint 4 test report to the repository. Once the backend is available, she will begin developing the Admin Panel frontend and integrating it with the backend APIs.

---

## Sprint Progress

Sprint 4 focuses on developing the Admin Panel, including product management, inventory management, customer order management, and customer account management. The backend implementation is progressing, with three major components completed and customer account management remaining. Automated frontend tests have been completed, while the test report and frontend development are the next priorities. The sprint remains in progress.

---

## Challenges and Solutions

The main challenge is the dependency of frontend development on backend delivery. To address this, Backend developer will complete customer account management and verify the backend functionality, while Faezah Ahmadi prepares the test report. Minor backend bugs were resolved independently, and no major unresolved technical blockers were reported.

---

## Meeting Outcome

The team reviewed the current Sprint 4 progress and agreed to prioritize completing and testing the remaining backend functionality. Frontend developer will submit the test report and begin frontend development once the backend is delivered. The team will continue coordinating to ensure smooth frontend–backend integration and progress toward completing the Admin Panel within the sprint.
