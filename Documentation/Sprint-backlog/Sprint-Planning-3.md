# Fresh Mart – Sprint 3 Planning

Project: E-Commerce Grocery Shop (Fresh Mart) 

Course: ITC 315 – Software Engineering Project Sprint: 3 

Sprint Duration: October 14–17, 2026 

Epic: Payment / Checkout + Orders 

Prepared by: Sayeda Taiba Agha (Product Owner)

## 1. Sprint Goal

Creating a fully functional and secure payment section for customers. Customers should be able to add their personal information in the payment page and have delivery and payment options to facilitate the process. At last in payment section, customers should be able to review the order, place the order, and view order history to track their orders.

## 5. Sprint 3 Expected Deliverables

The following features are expected to be developed by the development team in Sprint 3:

- Customers can provide contact and shipping details.
- Customers can select delivery and payment options.
- Customers can review their order before submission.
- Orders can be placed and saved successfully.
- Customers receive an order confirmation.
- Customers can access their previous orders.
- Checkout and order functionality is tested.
