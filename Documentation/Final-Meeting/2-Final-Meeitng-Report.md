# Final Project Review Meeting Agenda

Course: ITC 315 – Software Engineering Project
Project: E-Commerce Grocery Shop (Fresh Mart)
Meeting: Final Project Review Meeting
Date: October 25, 2026
Scrum Master: Zohal Riaz
Product Owner: Sayeda Taiba Agha

--------------------------------------------------
1. Meeting Objectives
--------------------------------------------------

- Present the final version of the Fresh Mart application.
- Demonstrate the main customer and admin functionalities.
- Review the completed project features.
- Verify frontend and backend integration.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Customer Registration and Login
--------------------------------------------------

- Demonstrate customer account creation and login.
- Verify email format and password validation.
- Show successful registration and login.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Product Catalog and Search
--------------------------------------------------

- Browse product categories and product details.
- Demonstrate product sorting by name and price.
- Demonstrate product search and category filtering.
- Filter products by availability and price range.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Shopping Cart Management
--------------------------------------------------

- Add products to the shopping cart.
- Increase and decrease product quantities.
- Verify automatic price and total updates.
- Demonstrate the clear-cart functionality.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Checkout and Order Placement
--------------------------------------------------

- Enter contact and shipping information.
- Select delivery and payment options.
- Review order details and the order summary.
- Place an order and display the order confirmation.
- Verify the order number and order status.

--------------------------------------------------
6. Admin Dashboard Overview
--------------------------------------------------

- Demonstrate the admin dashboard.
- Review product, order, and customer statistics.
- Display low-stock and out-of-stock products.
- Review the latest customer orders.

--------------------------------------------------
7. Product Management
--------------------------------------------------

- Add new products.
- Edit product names, prices, categories, and images.
- Delete products.
- Verify product changes in the customer catalog.

--------------------------------------------------
8. Inventory Management
--------------------------------------------------

- Update product stock quantities.
- Demonstrate low-stock and out-of-stock status.
- Verify inventory changes in the customer catalog.

--------------------------------------------------
9. Customer Order Management
--------------------------------------------------

- View customer orders in the admin panel.
- Update order statuses, including processing, shipped, and delivered.
- Verify order status changes in customer order history.

--------------------------------------------------
10. Customer Account Management
--------------------------------------------------

- View registered customer information.
- Search for customers.
- Demonstrate customer account management and blocking functionality.

--------------------------------------------------
11. Final Review and Discussion
--------------------------------------------------

- Review the application's main features.
- Discuss testing and frontend-backend integration.
- Identify any remaining issues or improvements.
- Collect feedback from the team and Product Owner.

--------------------------------------------------
12. Meeting Conclusion
--------------------------------------------------

- Summarize the completed project functionality.
- Review the final project deliverables.
- Conclude the final project review meeting.
