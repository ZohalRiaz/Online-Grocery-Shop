# E-commerce Grocery Shop (Kabul Fresh Mart)

Sayeda Taiba Agha (Product Owner)

## Project Overview

Kabul Fresh Mart is a web-based online platform for facilitating the search and purchase of grocery products over the phone at their convenience from their home. The system is organized around five main features: **Registration, Catalog/Search, Cart Management, Payment/Checkout, and Admin Panel.**

The goal of this project is to simplify daily shopping activities for people by providing an online platform where they can register their own account, look at the products that they want to buy, and have them delivered to their homes instead of going to physical stores.

In the Fresh Mart platform, customers will be able to find their needed products easily using the catalog/search feature. The platform is best for those who are struggling to manage their time for their regular shopping because the platform will facilitate their search with the categorization feature it will offer, while the delivery option will facilitate the proper delivery of the product to the correct location.

The cart management system will help customers process and reorganize all the products before they go to the actual payment section. Managing cash and calculating product prices can be another struggle that most of the customers face with on-site shopping, while our secure payment checkout section will have the ability to process those activities more precisely.

The order tracking feature will inform customers about what they purchased and how it’s progressing through the process.

In the admin panel, there will be a dedicated dashboard for the admin where they will be able to manage and organize products and inventory. Also, through this panel, they can see the product purchases and the customer information. This feature will provide all the management system for the admin into one dashboard where they can easily locate and mange their products.

Overall, Kabul Fresh Mart is a user-friendly platform which bring the products to your home with a few clicks on your phone.

## Project Objectives

The primary goal of the Fresh Mart system is to create a robust and easy-to-use grocery shopping application that facilitates the entire process of customer shopping right from the creation of their accounts to completing their orders.

1. Customer account management facility
2. Organization of products in the form of a product catalog
3. Easy-to-find product facility
4. Facility for managing the shopping cart
5. The checkout process facility
6. Ordering and order tracking facility
7. Facility for secure administration
8. Product and inventory management facility for administrators
9. Order and customer management facility for administrators
10. Integration facility
